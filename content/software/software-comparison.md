This table lists [XMPP Extensions (XEPs)](/extensions/) of Standards Track, Informational, and Historical types (excluding Deprecated, Obsolete, Retracted, and Rejected XEPs). Use the "Add App" button to compare [Compliance Suite](/about/compliance-suites/) levels and supported XEPs for individual clients, servers, libraries or tools. By default, only apps providing XEP information in their DOAP file are listed, and only XEPs listed by at least one of the selected apps are shown. Use the switches below the "Add App" button to show all apps or all XEPs. Selected apps are kept in the page address, so you can share a comparison by copying the link. Visit the [Software section](/software/) for filtering apps by operating system, compliance, and more.
