From 15th to 16th August 2026, XMPP Community members will be present with an XMPP booth at FrOSCon 2026, the Free and Open Source Software Conference!

As every year, the FrOSCon e.V. (club) organises together with the computer science department of the University of Applied Sciences Bonn-Rhein-Sieg in [Sankt Augustin, Germany](http://www.openstreetmap.org/?minlon=7.18247890472412&minlat=50.7794532775879&maxlon=7.18388938903809&maxlat=50.7800941467285).
The venue is free of cost and no registration is required.

Find the program and more details on their webpage: [froscon.org](https://froscon.org/en/)

Come by and have a personal chat with XMPP Community members and XMPP developers at the XMPP (Jabber) booth!

See you soon at FrOSCon 2026!
