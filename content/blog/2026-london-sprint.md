There will be a [XMPP Sprint in
London](https://wiki.xmpp.org/web/Sprints/2026-11_London) from the 6th to
the 8th of November at [The Mitre in
Greenwich](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/160665955).

The sprint is organised by [Matthew
Fennell](https://fennell.dev/social/matthew) and co-organised by
[Polarian](https://polarian.dev).

If you are interested in attending please do book quickly and add your
details onto the wiki page, if you do not have an account please contact one
of the organisers and we can do it for you:

* Matthew: [XMPP](xmpp:matthew@fennell.dev)
* Polarian: [XMPP](xmpp:polarian@icebound.dev)
  [Email](mailto:polarian@polarian.dev)

Also any operators interested in improving the operator space and would like
to attend the sprint please get into contact with Polarian, who is an XMPP
operator and interested in working on operator related issues.
