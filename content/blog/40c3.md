XMPP Community members will have an Assembly at the [40th Chaos Communication Congress (40C3)](https://events.ccc.de/congress/2026/infos/startpage.html) from the 27th to 30th December 2026 at [Hamburg Messehallen](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/8975932). For those who have attended previous Chaos Communication Congresses, do take note the venue has changed, and is **NOT** at CCH Hamburg.

The [Chaos Communication Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaos_Communication_Congress) is an annual hacker conference held in Germany. It is one of the biggest hacker conferences in the world.

If you are attending 40C3 come past the Assembly and hang out with XMPP community members, we have stickers!!!

Also, please consider joining the 40C3 XMPP group chat (MUC) if you are attending the conference to stay in contact with others attending the conference: [40c3@canale.nucile.ro](xmpp:40c3@canale.nucile.ro?join).
