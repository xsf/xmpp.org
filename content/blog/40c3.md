XMPP Community members will have an Assembly at [40C3](https://events.ccc.de/congress/2026/infos/startpage.html) from the 27th to 30th December 2026.

[Chaos Computing Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaos_Communication_Congress) is an annual hacker conference held in Germany. It is one of the biggest hacker conferences in the world.

Tickets are hard to get and pricy, for more information see the [tickets info page](https://events.ccc.de/congress/2026/infos/tickets/). If you are already attending 40C3 come past the Assembly and hang out with XMPP community members, we have stickers!!!

Also, please consider joining the 40C3 MUC if you are attending the conference to stay in contact with others attending the conference: [40c3@canale.nucile.ro](xmpp:40c3@canale.nucile.ro?join).
