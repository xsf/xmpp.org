[OpMeet](https://wiki.xmpp.org/web/OpMeet) are online meetups/conferences
targeted at XMPP Operators.

Due to a family health emergency, Polarian was unable to hold OpMeet on the
original date, and it was moved to the 31st October.

Due to severe sickness after the XMPP Stand in Romania, Polarian will be
unable to hold OpMeet 2026.10 altogether. Apologies to anyone who was
planning on attending, and for those who put their ideas forward, this was
unavoidable.

OpMeet will hopefully return hopefully in early 2027.
